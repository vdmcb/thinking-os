# Large-document procedure

Use this procedure whenever the complete source cannot be read safely in one context or contains many files, pages, slides, sheets, or appendices.

## 1. Build the inventory

Create a coverage ledger before detailed interpretation.

| Unit | Title or range | Type | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive summary | section | pending | |
| 2 | Pages 8-19 | pages | pending | Financial model |
| 3 | Revenue model | sheet | pending | Formulas and units |

Allowed status values:

- `read`
- `omitted`
- `uncertain`
- `pending`

Every source unit must end as `read`, `omitted`, or `uncertain`.

## 2. Partition semantically

Split by meaningful boundaries:

- headings and appendices;
- PDF chapters or stable page ranges;
- complete slides and their notes;
- spreadsheet sheets or coherent table regions;
- related exhibits and the claims that reference them.

Do not split in the middle of a table, slide, argument, or footnote chain merely to hit an arbitrary token count.

## 3. Produce section fragments

For each partition, capture:

- concrete topic;
- material claims;
- reasons or mechanisms;
- evidence and locators;
- assumptions;
- numbers and constraints;
- qualifications or dissent;
- unknowns and extraction gaps;
- dependencies on another partition.

Fragments are working notes, not final output.

## 4. Reconcile across sections

Before final synthesis:

- merge repeated claims;
- preserve actual conflicts;
- connect exhibits to the claims they support;
- resolve duplicated numbering carefully;
- distinguish a later correction from a contradiction;
- verify that every material number retains its unit and locator;
- check appendices for qualifications that alter the main text.

## 5. Complete the coverage ledger

Do not finalize while any unit remains `pending`.

If a unit is `omitted` or `uncertain`, record:

- why;
- its likely relevance;
- which conclusions might change if it were available.

Mark the final Understanding Packet incomplete when a missing unit could materially change the central idea or reasoning map.

## 6. Synthesize

Build one Understanding Packet from the fragments. Do not paste section summaries together. The final packet should expose the source's overall semantic structure while retaining material local differences and locators.
