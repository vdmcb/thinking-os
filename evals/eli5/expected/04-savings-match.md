# Company savings match - ELI5

## What this is

A rule about a savings plan at work. It says how much the company adds to what an employee saves, and when that added money becomes the employee's to keep.

## The basic facts

- An employee may put up to 4% of their monthly salary into the plan.
- For every 2 euros the employee puts in, the company adds 1 euro, but only on the first 4% of salary.
- The company's part becomes the employee's in three equal yearly pieces, counted from the day the employee started.

## How it works

1. Each month the employee chooses how much salary to put in.
2. The company adds half of that amount, up to the 4% cap.
3. Anything the employee puts in above 4% is allowed but gets no added money.
4. One year after the start date, one third of the company's part belongs to the employee for good.
5. The same happens after two years and after three years, so after three years all of it is theirs.
6. An employee who leaves before one of those dates loses the company's part that has not yet become theirs.
7. The employee's own money is always theirs, whenever they leave.

## Why it is this way

The file says the match exists to encourage saving for the long term and to reward staying with the company. It does not say why the cap is 4% or why the wait is three years.

## Words you will see

- vests: becomes the employee's to keep.
- forfeits: loses.
