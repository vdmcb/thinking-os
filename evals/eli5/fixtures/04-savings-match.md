# Company savings match

Employees may put up to 4% of their monthly salary into the savings plan. The company adds 50% of whatever the employee puts in, up to that 4% cap. Contributions above 4% are allowed but are not matched.

The company's part vests one third per year over three years, counted from the employee's start date. An employee who leaves before a vesting date forfeits the unvested company part. The employee's own contributions are always theirs.

The match exists to encourage long-term saving and to reward staying with the company.
