# Northstar Fulfilment — Capacity Decision

## Approval requested

Authorize $2.4 million for an automated packing cell and a five-person implementation team.

## Options considered

The memo says the options were assessed on throughput, cost, implementation time, and disruption.

| Option | Throughput | Cost | Implementation time | Disruption |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Repair the current line | 1,100 parcels per shift | $420,000 repair price | 10 weeks | High |
| Lease overflow capacity | Not stated | $38,000 per month | 4 weeks | Medium |
| Build the automated cell | 2,400 parcels per shift | $2.4 million project budget | 16 weeks | Low |

The current-line throughput is a measured four-week average. The repair figure is a contractor quote that excludes weekend installation and lost production. The lease figure covers rent only; handling, transport, staffing, and the required lease term are not included. The automated-cell throughput is a vendor target from a demonstration facility, and its budget includes equipment and implementation labor but excludes building reinforcement and maintenance.

## Recommendation

The source recommends the automated cell because it says it delivers the best throughput, lowest long-run cost, and least operational disruption. It provides no demand forecast, common operating period, total-cost calculation, disruption definition, weighting method, or evidence that the demonstration-facility throughput applies to this warehouse and parcel mix.
