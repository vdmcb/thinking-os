# Atlas Records Service — Scale Plan

## Approval requested

Approve an eight-person service unit for the next 12 months. Annual payroll is budgeted at $960,000.

## Current book

As of 30 June, the service has 12 contracted customers and $480,000 of annual recurring revenue. The current team can put 12 new customers into production per year.

## Growth case

The planning case assumes five new contracts per quarter and 122% net revenue retention. The expanded unit will remove the current implementation constraint.

The planning model forecasts:

| Measure | Month 12 | Month 36 |
|---|---:|---:|
| ARR | $1.74 million | $4.36 million |
| Operating profit | $90,000 | $2.05 million |

These figures will establish a durable recurring-revenue engine. The model does not show customer cohort timing, average contract value, churn and expansion components, revenue-recognition timing, service cost per customer, implementation capacity after hiring, or formulas connecting the stated assumptions to the outputs.
