# Lumen Billing Cutover

## Decision requested

Authorize the retirement of the legacy billing workflow on 1 October and move all enterprise invoices to Lumen.

## Claimed operating result

The source says the cutover will reduce invoice corrections from 7% to below 2% within 90 days. The migration team will map customer records, finance will validate opening balances, and account teams will notify customers.

## Control model

A weekly dashboard will show correction rate, unmatched balances, and customer complaints. The transformation council will review the dashboard every Friday. Unmatched balances above CHF 250,000 are marked red.

The document does not identify who is accountable for the below-2% result; who has authority to pause the cutover, accept a balance discrepancy, or require remediation; who must act on a red threshold; or how unresolved issues escalate before the legacy workflow is retired.
