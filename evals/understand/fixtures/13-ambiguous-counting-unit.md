# Coastline Equipment Inspection Expansion

## Approval requested

Authorize three implementation specialists for 12 months at a loaded cost of $420,000. The source says this capacity will support 30 deployments and $720,000 of annual recurring fees by year end.

## Reusable library

The service currently has six inspection blueprints. A blueprint is a reusable configuration for one equipment class.

## Operating snapshot

Eighteen customer sites are under contract. Eleven configured inspections are live across those sites. Some sites use more than one blueprint, and the same blueprint can be installed at multiple sites.

## Capacity and revenue case

The source says each specialist can complete ten deployments per year. In the library section, `deployment` refers to publishing a reusable blueprint; in the operating snapshot, it refers to a configured inspection running at a customer site; and in the implementation plan, it refers to a completed commissioning event, including replacements at existing sites.

The $720,000 forecast multiplies 30 deployments by $24,000 per deployment. The source does not define which deployment unit is billable, reconcile blueprints, live inspections, sites, and commissioning events, or state whether replacements and multiple installations at one site are counted more than once.
