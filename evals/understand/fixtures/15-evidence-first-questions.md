# Halcyon Support Deflection Bot: Funding Proposal

## Ask

We request EUR 120,000 to build and launch an AI support chatbot in Q1, deflecting routine tickets before they reach the support team.

## Demand

Customers are asking for this. Three enterprise customers raised the topic of self-service automation in the last quarter, and industry adoption of support AI is accelerating.

## Savings

Vendor case studies show 40% ticket deflection is achievable; Zendra's published case study reports 42% at a comparable SaaS company. Applied to our support budget of EUR 750,000, a 40% deflection rate yields roughly EUR 300,000 in annual savings. The bot pays for itself in five months.

## Delivery

The platform team can absorb the build alongside current commitments. No additional headcount is requested.
