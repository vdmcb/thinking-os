# Meridian Workflow Service — Launch Case

## Approval requested

Authorize a seven-person managed-workflow unit for 12 months with payroll of $840,000.

## Buyer evidence

An external survey of 310 operations leaders at large North American companies reports that 72% prefer a fixed monthly price to pure usage pricing and 64% rank budget predictability as a top-three concern. The citation is listed, but the underlying study was not included.

## Commercial case

The source says this preference validates the proposed fixed-base-plus-usage package for Nordic mid-market manufacturers. It therefore forecasts 15 signed customers in year one, 125% net revenue retention in year two, and 65% gross margin.

No evidence is provided for willingness to pay at the proposed price, conversion from preference to purchase, the acquisition funnel, renewal or expansion behavior, delivery cost, or applicability from large North American companies to Nordic mid-market manufacturers.
