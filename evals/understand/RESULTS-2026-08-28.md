# Understand 2.1 live trial, 2026-08-28

Five private documents, run with the 2.1.0 skill as merged in PR #3 plus the three-round audit cap from this change. Sources, outputs, and audit transcripts are not committed; they live in `evals/understand/private/` (gitignored) on the machine that ran the trial. What follows is derived: lints, audit counts, and rubric scores. The lints and audit counts are objective and reproducible from the private files. The five outputs and the five extracted sources are the artifacts behind every number here. The rubric scores are model-judged by a single rater (the operator of the run), with no second rater, no calibration, and no human verification; they are a secondary record and should not inherit the credibility of the earlier blind format trial, which had a human picking. Rubric dimension 14 (run and follow-up) is unscored. Provenance differs per document and is stated under "How the runs went": none of D1 to D4 ran end-to-end under the capped skill.

## How the runs went

- D1, D2, D4 (provenance: transcript drafts, retrofitted audit): run by subagents executing SKILL.md 2.1.0 as committed in PR #3, before the cap existed. Each subagent's own audit loop did not converge before the runs were cut off by account spend limits: D1 reached its 27th draft, D4 its ninth audit round, D2 its 16th draft. The latest draft of each was taken as the run output and then given fresh, independent audits under the three-round cap. These outputs therefore show what the uncapped skill drafted plus what a capped audit fixed, not an end-to-end capped run.
- D3 (provenance: in-session draft, capped audit): drafted by the operator after its subagent left no output, then audited under the cap.
- D5 (provenance: in-session, end-to-end): drafted and audited in-session by the operator with the installed 2.1.0 skill; three audit rounds, the last clean.
- The uncapped loop is the reason for the cap in this change. A strict auditor ("prefer a false alarm") always finds one more qualifier; without a stop rule the loop consumes the budget without a final output.

Rubric summary: every dimension is at or above 3 and every average is above 4.0. On the decision-critical minimum (faithfulness, epistemic separation, quantitative integrity, traceability each at least 4), D1 and D3 fail on traceability, and that dimension should be read as known-failing for HTML sources in this trial: the sources have no page numbers, and neither output cites section headings instead. That was a skill gap, not a document gap. This change adds the rule to core/references/reading.md (a page-less source is cited by its nearest heading, table caption, or list item); the D1 and D3 outputs predate the rule and were not regenerated.

## Follow-ups this trial produced

1. Locators for sources without pages: done in this change (core/references/reading.md names the fallback: nearest heading, table caption, or list item). Not yet verified on a regenerated HTML output.
2. Clipped sources: the "quote only intact text" rule held once applied, but the subagent reconstructed ten clipped words before the audit caught them. The rule may need to sit in step 3 (ledger) as well as step 2, so the ledger records the cut.
3. Length cuts are a fault source of their own: two of the D4 and D5 findings were silences overstated by trimming words to meet the ceiling. The compression pass in output-format.md should say that a cut which removes a qualifier is a fact change and re-enters the audit.
