# Reference: Ambiguous metrics

## In one sentence

The CSV contains two scenarios with revenue values 1.4 and 2.1 and growth values 18 and 24, but supplies no currency, scale, percentage signs, periods, or scenario definitions.

## Required distinctions

- Preserve every displayed value exactly.
- Do not add dollars, euros, millions, percentages, years, or forecasts.
- Units and periods are unknown, not safely inferable.
- Ask what units and periods apply, explaining that the answer determines how every metric should be interpreted.
