# Reference: Conversion pilot

## In one sentence

The source reports conversion rising from 8% to 12% over six weeks among 220 account-manager-selected existing customers, but provides no control result and does not establish generalization to new or self-serve customers.

## Required distinctions

- The four-percentage-point increase is a source observation, not proof of causality.
- Selection by account managers is a material qualification.
- Exclusion of new and self-serve customers limits generalization.
- Missing control results and selection criteria are unknowns.
- Do not recommend rollout or rejection.
