# Halcyon Support Deflection Bot - Simplified

This proposal asks for EUR 120,000 to build an AI support chatbot in Q1, projecting roughly EUR 300,000 in annual savings from deflecting 40% of routine tickets (Ask; Savings).

Both load-bearing numbers come from outside the company. The 40% deflection rate is borrowed from a vendor's published case study of a different company, not measured against Halcyon's own tickets, and the EUR 300,000 saving is that borrowed rate applied to the EUR 750,000 support budget (Savings). The demand evidence is three enterprise customers who "raised the topic" of self-service automation, which is not a request for this product (Demand). The claim that the platform team can absorb the build alongside current commitments is asserted without a capacity basis (Delivery).

## Questions for the author

1. Has anyone measured what share of our own tickets is actually deflectable, and if not, what is the plan for measuring it before committing the EUR 120,000, and by when? (Savings)
2. What exactly did the three customers ask for? Raising a topic and requesting a product are different signals. (Demand)
3. What does the EUR 300,000 saving assume happens to support staffing: reduced hiring, reassignment, or something else? (Savings)
4. What current commitments does the platform team drop or delay to absorb this build? (Delivery)
