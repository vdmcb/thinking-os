# Meridian FY27 Platform Expansion - Simplified

This business case asks leadership to approve $2.4M in FY27 to replace dispatch tooling with the Atlas platform across all 14 regions, promising a 22% cut in cost per completed work order and $4.7M in annual savings from FY28 (executive summary; financial model).

The whole economic case extrapolates one 90-day pilot in the Southwest region to the entire company. That region was chosen for having the highest tooling-adoption scores, it gained two large commercial contracts mid-pilot that shifted its job mix, and nine of the fourteen regions score below it on readiness (pilot section; appendix E). Three more weak points. The model books full savings one quarter after go-live in every region, with nothing from the pilot bearing on how fast other regions ramp (financial model). Integration for the three regions running customized builds was never separately estimated; a shared $150,000 contingency covers all workstreams (implementation plan). And nobody is named as accountable for the 22% target or authorized to pause the rollout if it does not materialize (organizational readiness). The vendor comparison also mixes unlike sources: Atlas's own proposal, a 2024 analyst report for Fieldly, and an internal estimate for DispatchPro (vendor assessment).

## Questions for the author

1. What did the pilot's cost reduction look like without the two large commercial contracts added mid-pilot? That isolates what Atlas itself delivered. (pilot section)
2. Is there measured evidence from any region resembling the other thirteen, and if not, what is the plan for validating the savings before full rollout, and by when? (appendix E)
3. What is the separately estimated integration cost for the three customized regions? A shared $150,000 contingency is not an estimate. (implementation plan)
4. Who is accountable for the 22% target, and at what measured shortfall, by what date, does the rollout pause? (financial model; organizational readiness)
5. Can the vendor comparison be redone with every cell from the same kind of source, unknown cells left blank? (vendor assessment)

Held: the Southwest pilot data behind the 22% figure, the arithmetic from that pilot to the $4.7M, the vendor comparison by source, and the page map. Ask for any of them.
