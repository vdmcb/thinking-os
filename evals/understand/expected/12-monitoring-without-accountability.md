# Reference: Lumen billing cutover accountability

## In one sentence

The source asks the reviewer to authorize a dated billing cutover and claims a lower correction rate, but its dashboard, threshold, and review cadence do not assign authority or accountability for acting when the migration departs from plan.

## Required distinctions

- Mapping records, validating balances, and notifying customers are assigned activities; they do not identify who is accountable for the end-to-end cutover result.
- A named transformation council and Friday review cadence establish monitoring, not decision rights.
- The CHF 250,000 red threshold is observable, but the source does not state the required action, authorized decision-maker, or escalation path.
- The output must not infer that finance, the migration team, account teams, or the council owns the result or has pause, exception, or remediation authority.
- The output must not recommend a different governance model or whether to authorize the cutover.

## Material pushback

Request an accountability and exception matrix tied to the 7%-to-below-2% claim and CHF 250,000 threshold. It must name the single accountable owner for the result, the owners and decision rights for pause, discrepancy acceptance, and remediation, the required action when the threshold is red, and the escalation path and deadline before retirement. Explain that this is needed to determine whether the stated controls can govern the cutover rather than merely report it.
