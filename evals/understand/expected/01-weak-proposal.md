# Reference: Growth Platform Proposal

## In one sentence

The source requests €900,000 and six people for two quarters to build an undefined AI platform that it claims will reduce processing costs by 40%, without supplying a baseline, mechanism, scope, or supporting evidence.

## Required distinctions

- The team size, schedule, budget, and claimed reduction are source statements.
- None of the delivery feasibility, savings, or moat claims is established by evidence in the fixture.
- “Platform,” “transformation layer,” “operational leverage,” and “strategic moat” require concrete definitions.
- The output must not recommend a pilot, approval, rejection, or another architecture.

## Material author question

Ask which baseline and calculation produce the 40% reduction, explaining that this resolves the proposal's central economic claim.
