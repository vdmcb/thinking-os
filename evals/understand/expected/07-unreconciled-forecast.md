# Reference: Atlas Records Service scale plan

## In one sentence

The source asks for a $960,000 eight-person unit for 12 months on the strength of a planning model that starts from 12 contracted customers and $480,000 ARR and forecasts $1.74 million ARR at month 12 and $4.36 million at month 36, but does not expose a reproducible bridge from stated growth assumptions to revenue, capacity, cost, or profit.

## Required distinctions

- The 12 contracts and $480,000 ARR are actuals reported as of 30 June; they are not forecast outputs.
- Five new contracts per quarter and 122% NRR are model assumptions, not observed growth or independently supported evidence.
- Month-12 and month-36 ARR and operating profit are model outputs, not commitments or actual performance.
- Stating inputs beside outputs does not make the forecast reproducible or internally reconciled.
- The source does not establish that the expanded team removes the current capacity constraint.
- The output must preserve the $960,000, eight-person, and 12-month commitment boundary without recommending approval or rejection.

## Required reasoning and reconciliation

- Reconstruct the load-bearing chain as `fund team → increase implementation capacity → add and retain/expand customer cohorts → recognize ARR → cover service and fixed costs → produce operating profit`.
- Mark every unsupported or absent bridge in that chain rather than accepting the terminal forecast as support for the ask.
- State that the forecast cannot be recalculated from the visible material because cohort timing, contract value, NRR decomposition, recognition timing, service costs, post-hiring capacity, and formulas are absent.
- Do not invent a growth formula, infer that 122% NRR applies uniformly, or calculate missing outputs from guessed conventions.

## Material pushback

Request one auditable forecast bridge tying the 30 June actual base through customer cohorts, implementation capacity, contract values, churn/expansion, revenue timing, service and fixed costs, and formulas to each stated ARR and profit output. Explain that this determines whether the requested team plausibly produces the claimed economics, rather than merely restating the model's result.
