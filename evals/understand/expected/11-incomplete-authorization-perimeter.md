# Reference: Northstar service desk authorization

## In one sentence

The source asks the reviewer to authorize a six-role service desk, but leaves the actual commitment boundary unresolved because it does not specify how the roles are sourced, their loaded cost and funding, the authorization period, or any associated spending authority.

## Required distinctions

- Authorization of the service desk and assignment of six roles are explicit requests.
- Use of the existing workflow platform and service to regional operations teams are stated scope conditions, not separate approvals.
- Shorter exception handling and released local capacity are unsupported source claims, not established outcomes.
- A named monthly reviewer does not resolve who controls staffing, funding, or exceptions to the authorization.
- The output must not silently translate six roles into six incremental hires or assume the platform has no incremental cost.
- The output must not recommend approval, rejection, a smaller team, or a pilot.

## Required authorization analysis

- Reconstruct the authorization perimeter by separating the explicit decision from implied commitments and unresolved commitment dimensions.
- State that role sourcing, loaded cost, funding source, start date, duration, and ancillary spending authority are unknown.
- Do not present the ask as fully specified merely because the team composition is stated.

## Material pushback

Request a single authorization schedule that distinguishes hires, transfers, and temporary allocations and states loaded cost, funding source, effective date, duration, and included vendor or platform authority. Explain that this is needed to determine what organizational and financial commitment the approval would actually create.
